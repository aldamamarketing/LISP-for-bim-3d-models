## 2025-05-18 - Prevent Over-fetching on Firestore document queries
**Learning:** When retrieving specific user-favorite documents from the `publicAssets` collection in Firestore, querying for `where('type', '==', type)` and returning the entire set just to filter it locally by `favIds` creates an unnecessary performance bottleneck that scales poorly. Attempting to use `where(documentId(), 'in', favIds)` along with `where('type', '==', type)` requires composite indexes that can be tedious to manage or error-prone.
**Action:** Chunk the array of `favIds` into batches (e.g. 30 documents max) and query using `where(documentId(), 'in', chunk)`. Then perform the filtering by `type` client-side on the small returned batch. This avoids over-fetching data, avoids composite index requirements, and scales properly since it only fetches what's explicitly needed in O(N) where N is the size of the user's favorites, rather than O(M) where M is the size of the whole collection.
