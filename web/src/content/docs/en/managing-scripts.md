---
title: "Managing Scripts"
description: "Understand how to manage your scripts, edit menus, organize groups, and control licenses."
icon: "dashboard"
---
Your .lsp files are securely stored in the cloud and downloaded into memory only when needed (JIT).

- **Upload:** In the Portal, go to LISP Manager and click '+ Add LISPs'. You can select multiple files at once.
- **Edit Metadata:** Click the pencil icon next to a routine to change its Friendly Name, Suite, and Icon.
- **Remove:** Click the trash icon to delete the routine from your Workspace. Changes reflect in AutoCAD on the next load.
